01 / About

A closer look at what the model actually sees.

Cancaria is an experimental image-classification platform for lung CT frames. It studies a narrow question: how does a small convolutional network distribute its confidence across four supplied classes?

Most medical-AI demonstrations stop at the confident answer. Cancaria starts there. Every run exposes the full four-class distribution, the transform that produced the input, the checkpoint behind the result, and a plain account of what the output cannot support.

The platform is made for medical-imaging researchers, students, and anyone examining how machine learning behaves near the edge of clinical use. It is not a screening service, a diagnostic device, or a second opinion.

A softmax score is a distribution over four labels. It is not a probability that a person has a disease.

02 / Workflow

From one frame to one record.

The pipeline is deliberately small. Nothing is inferred beyond the four labels, and nothing is generated that the model did not return.

Step 01 / Input PNG · JPEG · WebP

Drop a CT frame

or choose a file · up to 10 MB

File type, size, and decodability are checked in the browser and again on the server. Samples are presented without labels.

Step 02 / Transform Resize · crop · normalise
256 px Short side resized
224 × 224 Centre crop
μ / σ ImageNet normalisation

This is the exact transform declared with the checkpoint: resize, centre crop, tensor conversion, and normalisation. No augmentation is applied at inference.
